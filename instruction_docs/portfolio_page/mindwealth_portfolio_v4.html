How "Normal" regime is determined
We download 3 years of daily VIX closes (Yahoo: ^VIX).
Today VIX = 16.4 → sits at the 40th percentile of that 3yr window.

3yr percentile → Regime:
Below 25th pct → Low Volatility
25th–75th pct → Normal ← you are here
Above 75th pct → Stress

Why 3 years? It captures a full market cycle (bull + correction + recovery) without being distorted by a single extreme event. Full-history percentiles can be anchored to 2020 COVID spikes and skew everything.

Each cell shows the average correlation between all stocks in those two investment types. Pairs breaching ρ >0.85 appear in the list below — those are the only ones requiring action.
