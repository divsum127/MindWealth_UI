## ANALYSIS_DATE AND METHODOLOGY

**ANALYSIS_DATE: 2026-05-20**

**Determination:** Derived from "Today Trading Date/Price[$]" field consistently showing 2026-05-20 across all payload rows in all 8 parallel processing groups.

**"Today's price" definition:** For each signal, the price on 2026-05-20 from the matching interval series (Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Quarterly) as reported in "Today Trading Date/Price[$]" field.

**Timeliness Assessment Methodology:**
- **Elapsed time:** Calculated from Signal Date to ANALYSIS_DATE (2026-05-20)
- **Price distance:** Current price vs Signal Price, Signal Open Price, Breakout Price, Confirmed Price (as available)
- **Progress:** (Current Price - Entry Anchor) / (First Target - Entry Anchor), when computable
- **Lateness threshold:** Progress ≥60% toward primary target range, OR holding period exceeds average historical winning hold by >50% while in drawdown
- **Waiver:** Lateness waived if rr_to_nearest_support_stop ≥3.0× (rarely available in payload)

**CAGR Alpha Interpretation:**
- **Positive alpha:** Strategy CAGR > Buy & Hold CAGR → strategy adds value
- **Negative alpha:** Strategy CAGR < Buy & Hold CAGR → strategy underperforms passive holding
- **For shorts:** Payload does not explicitly define short benchmark; reported CAGR alpha echoed as-is without reinterpretation
